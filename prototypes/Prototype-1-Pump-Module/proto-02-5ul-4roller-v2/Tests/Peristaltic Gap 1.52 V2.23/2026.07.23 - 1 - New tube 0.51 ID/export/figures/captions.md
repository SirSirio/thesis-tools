# Suggested figure captions

**accuracy_vs_strokes** — Per-stroke delivered volume versus stroke count (log axis) for each speed. Error bars are \pm1 SD of the per-stroke volume; the dashed line is the mean calibration slope, the dotted line the nominal value. The single-stroke points sit below the asymptote (priming deficit).

**per_stroke_vs_speed** — Per-stroke delivered volume versus pump speed at the compliant 100- and 300-stroke levels. Error bars are \pm1 SD. Volume decreases by ~2% from 60 to 240 RPM (incomplete refill at higher speed).

**cv_vs_speed** — Coefficient of variation versus pump speed at the 100- and 300-stroke levels; the dotted line is the reference pipette (0.27% CV at 50 µL). Precision is best near 180 RPM.

**cv_vs_volume** — Coefficient of variation versus delivered volume (log–log), coloured by speed. The dashed line is the ideal $1/\sqrt{N}$ (slope $-0.5$) scaling; the dotted line is the reference pipette. The near-$-0.5$ slope indicates random, self-averaging per-stroke variability.

**evaporation** — Independent evaporation calibration: mass loss versus time with a linear fit giving the evaporation rate E used to correct every gravimetric reading.
