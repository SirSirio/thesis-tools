Prototype 2 · Module

Alignment Module

Hosts the sample rack and indexes each tube under the dispensing nozzle — it moves the samples, not the head.

V2 ✅
V2 · built 2026-06-25

Rack indexing stage

A rack-and-pinion stage that carries a custom 8-tube rack under the nozzle. Motor and pusher sit above the rail, so gravity — not a seal — keeps spilled liquid away from the parts that are hard to clean. Indexes reliably, but open-loop: it counts steps from wherever it happens to be.

8 tube positions · 22 mm pitch pinion Ø 12.80 mm ~140 mm stroke — 14 mm short of 154

V2.1 · bench-validated 2026-07-31

Homing — the stage learns where zero is

A roller-lever endstop, wired so that a severed cable reads as “at the switch” and halts the axis instead of driving it into a hard stop, plus a three-pass homing sequence that is fast where speed is free and slow only where the zero is actually made. Axis 1 measured on the bench; axis 2 never wired.

repeatable zero no worse than 0.03 mm 102 half-steps/mm 22 s full home, from 110 s
