Prototype 2 · Module

Alignment Module

Hosts the sample rack and indexes each tube under the dispensing nozzle — it moves the samples, not the head.

V2.1 ✅
V2 · built 2026-06-25 · V2.1 tested 2026-07-31

Rack indexing stage, with homing

A rack-and-pinion stage that carries a custom 8-tube rack under the nozzle. Motor and pusher sit above the rail, so gravity — not a seal — keeps spilled liquid away from the parts that are hard to clean. V2.1 bolts on a roller-lever endstop — wired so a severed cable reads as “at the switch” and halts the axis rather than driving it into a hard stop — plus three-pass homing. The stage stops counting steps from wherever it happens to be and finds its own zero.

8 tube positions · 22 mm pitch repeatable zero no worse than 0.03 mm 22 s full home, from 110 s ~140 mm stroke — 14 mm short of 154
