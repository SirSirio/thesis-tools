Operator Interface Prototypes

Nine candidate operator interfaces for the dispensing instrument, drawn as self-auditing web pages at the panel's real 320 × 240 before any firmware existed. These are the actual pages the design decisions were made on, published unchanged: each round below opens the artifact exactly as it was frozen, navigable where it was navigable then.

01

Why these are instruments, not mockups

A static picture can only assert that a screen works. The three design rounds measure themselves instead: a controls panel re-renders every candidate under four probes, and an audit recomputes on each render.

touch targets

Outlines every interactive region and flags anything under 44 px.

RGB565

Re-quantises every colour to the panel's real 5-6-5 bit depth before judging it.

sunlight

A glare wash approximating bright ambient light on the panel.

device dark

The on-device theme, a runtime toggle on the chosen design.

The audit panel beside each screen reports element count, smallest touch target, count under 44 px, recorded exceptions, and the worst text contrast after quantisation. The two home-screen studies work differently: they are comparison boards, carrying machine-state switches (position unknown, liquid levels, colour treatment) instead of an audit, so layout and colour can be judged under the states that matter.

02

Designing the interface

Three rounds between 2026-07-31 and 2026-08-09: nine candidates, one survivor. The rounds narrow from style, to structure, to the exact pixels.

Round 1 · 2026-07-31

Five candidates, static screens

How much of the interface should change? Three candidates explore layout and visual language; two restructure what the interface is about. Instrument, Guided run and Machine state survive the round.

It also settled a hardware fact: the panel is not sunlight-readable under any design, which fixed the dark-on-light polarity everything later inherits.

Round 2 · 2026-08-09

Three candidates, fully navigable

Round 1 compared pictures; round 2 compares interfaces: the whole 20-screen graph, clickable, one information architecture against another. Guided wins on its home layout, without the wizard.

The rack screen also produced a genuine negative result: no published design system documents a button-only grid subset selection, so that screen is a design contribution of its own.

Round 3 · frozen 2026-08-09

The chosen design

Guided's layout without the wizard, refined through four review passes with the operator. The changes are about language and honesty rather than layout: jargon replaced (PRIME became Fill tubing, HOMING became Reset position), volumes quantised to what the pump can actually deliver, and three distinct unknown states instead of optimistic defaults.

This page is the pixel contract the firmware was built from; the LVGL implementation transcribes it. Light and dark, both navigable.

03

Revisiting the home screen

These two studies are not steps of the same arc. They came on 2026-08-11, after the interface had been built, flashed and used, when the operator's own judgement, not a defect, reopened one screen: Home. Both are comparison boards drawn from the firmware's real token values and geometry, at 2× zoom.

Round 4 · 2026-08-11

Home screen colour study

Three colour treatments built from the ColorBrewer Dark2 palette the product already ships, drawn beside a recreation of the shipped screen as a fidelity benchmark. Colour rails was chosen and carried forward.

Round 5 · 2026-08-11

Home screen structural study

Three layouts for a home screen whose contents had changed, with colour treatment, liquid state and position warning as live switches so structure and colour could be judged independently. The chosen panel is marked; it was built and flashed the same day.

04

Provenance

The five pages are copied verbatim from the thesis design record and are published as dated artifacts: fixing even a typo inside a candidate would change the evidence of what was considered and when. Exactly two mechanical changes were made for publication: the two home-screen studies load a locally vendored copy of Montserrat instead of Google Fonts, so they render pixel-identically offline, and each page carries a small link back to this index. Every design decision, every measured value and every recorded exception is untouched; the full rationale is in SPEC.md beside this page.

The rounds open as they were drawn: light, instrument-grade pages, deliberately outside this site's visual language. Chapter 9 of the thesis (User Interface Module) cites this page by printed address, so its path is frozen.
