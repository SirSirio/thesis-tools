Colour is never the only signal

  • Channel palette is ColorBrewer Dark2, a colour-blind-safe qualitative set: #1B9E77 #D95F02 #7570B3 #E7298A #66A61E #E6AB02. Every swatch also carries its channel numeral, which is the encoding that survives any colour vision deficiency — WCAG 1.4.1 forbids colour as the sole differentiator, and ~8% of men have a deficiency.
  • Level status is a traffic light plus a word. Under 15% red, 15–33% amber, above green. Green/amber/red for consumable level is common practice, not a standard — IEC 60601-1-8 covers alarm priority and ISO 3864 covers safety signage; neither mandates this. Cite it as convention.
  • Two different traffic lights, deliberately. On Levels it measures the bottle against itself. On the pre-run check it measures the bottle against what this run needs — green enough, amber within 10%, red short. Red blocks the start.
