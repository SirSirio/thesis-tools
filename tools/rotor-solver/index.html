Rotor Geometry Solver

Solves rotor radius analytically so each roller pass delivers your target stroke volume. Occlusion loss is compensated by extending the required arc. Assumes 180° contact. Feasibility is checked against three independent constraints: roller collision, hub clearance, and available tube length.

² Dispense time for 1000 µL at the set step rate, 1⁄32 microstepping, 6400 steps/rev, excludes accel/decel.
Arc gap = arc between rollers − bearing OD; must be > 0 (rollers must not touch).
Hub clr = (R − roller radius) − boss radius; must be > 0 (roller must clear the motor boss).
Tube arc = πR; must be ≤ 123 mm (153 mm stop-to-stop − 2×15 mm straight runs), else tube too short.
R rounded to 0.1 mm (Bambu P1S resolution); PLA for first test prints.
