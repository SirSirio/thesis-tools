Rotor Geometry Solver

Solves rotor radius analytically so each roller pass delivers your target stroke volume. Occlusion loss is compensated by extending the required arc. Assumes 180° contact. Feasibility is checked against three independent constraints: roller collision, hub clearance, and available tube length.

¹ Geometric swept volume per stroke = delivered ÷ occlusion efficiency. This is the raw lumen volume the arc must sweep; after occlusion loss it delivers your target.
² Dispense time for 1000 µL at the set step rate, at the selected microstepping mode, excludes accel/decel.
Arc gap = arc between rollers − bearing OD; must be > 0 (rollers must not touch).
Hub clr = (R − roller radius) − boss radius; must be > 0 (roller must clear the motor boss).
Tube arc = πR; must be ≤ 300 mm (adjustable stoppers), else tube too short.
R rounded to 0.1 mm (Bambu P1S resolution); PLA for first test prints.
³ Factor of Safety (FoS) = torque at rim ÷ (200 g × rollers in contact). Green ≥ 2.0 · amber 1.0–2.0 · red < 1.0. Torque at rim (g) = 4800 × torque_fraction ÷ R_mm × 10.
&sup4; Max step rate (inductive ceiling, theoretical lower bound) = V_supply ÷ (2 × L × I_rated). At 12 V → 1333 steps/s; at 24 V → 2667 steps/s. DRV8825 chopper regulation enables higher practical rates.
