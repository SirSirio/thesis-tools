Timeline · Feb-Sep 2026

Reading the plan

Pump module You

The single-channel design is being finalised. Next is a 4-channel build for four liquids, enough to validate the multi-liquid concept, followed by a design-only scale study toward the eventual 6-channel target.

Pump test method You

You own the pump's test methodology and built the test-campaign software for it. That tool exists; the remaining work is running the validation campaign and folding results into the design.

Alignment module You

Currently an MVP. The next step is designing a queueing system, the largest unknown, then printing and assembling parts. Budgeted generously because the queueing logic may iterate.

Other modules Partner

Storage, user interface, electronics, and operate-area modules were carried mostly by your thesis partner and are largely prototyped. They feed into integration rather than needing new design from you.

Integration Shared

No full integration has happened yet: the module work ran longer than planned. The first joint module integration & testing run is the critical shared milestone before writing locks in.

Writing You

Much of the content already exists across these tools and prototype logs; the ~4-week sprint is mostly transferring documentation into the LaTeX thesis, not researching from scratch.
