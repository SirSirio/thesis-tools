# Plan 10-04 — Alignment module subpage (new content)

**Wave:** 1 (parallel with 10-02, 10-03)
**Creates:** `prototypes/Prototype-2-Alignment-Module/index.html`
**Modifies:** nothing

## Goal

Build the alignment module's detail page from scratch. Unlike 10-02 and 10-03 this is not a
move — it is new authoring, from a source document that already exists and is authoritative.

## Source — the only source

`prototypes/Prototype-2-Alignment-Module/PROTOTYPE.md` (rewritten 2026-07-31 from the
firmware repository's `hardware.md`, `bench_align.cpp` and `project-log.md`).

**No number may be invented.** If a value is not in that file, it does not go on the page.
Values the source marks as *not measured* — rail voltage under load, coil resistances, axis
2 entirely, the travel-budget negative control — must appear on the page **as gaps**, not be
quietly dropped. The honesty of the record is part of what makes it citable.

**No internal shorthand codes in reader-facing prose.** Spell the concept out. Requirement
identifiers from the firmware repository do not belong on this page.

## Tasks

1. Page shell, **two** levels below root: `../../assets/style.css`,
   `../../assets/prototype-page.css`. Back-link `../index.html` → `← Prototype Design Space`.
   Title: `Alignment Module · rack indexing stage — Prototype Design Space`.
2. Root element `class="proto-page proto-page--align"` — this is what makes the page violet
   instead of orange. Everything downstream inherits from that one class.
3. `.proto-page-header`: eyebrow "Prototype 2 · Alignment Module", title, status chips
   (`V2 built`, `V2.1 bench-validated`, and a warning chip for the unmet stroke criterion),
   and a one-line summary of what the module does.
4. **Hero figure**: `AlignmentModuelHomingV2.1.png`, bare filename, with the caption from
   §5 of the source naming what is visible — the microswitch at the left end of the rail, the
   stepper on its bracket with the pinion engaging the printed rack, the tube rack on the
   carriage. Give it a `loading="lazy"` and sensible `width`/`height` so layout does not
   jump; it is a 3 MB file.
5. Sections, following the source document's structure:
   - **What it does** (§1) and **design priorities / gravity-protected layout** (§3)
   - **Targets & pass criteria** (§2) — as a table, with the unmet stroke row visibly flagged
   - **Mechanism as built** (§4) including the resolution derivation. Render the
     half-steps-per-mm arithmetic as a display equation; use the KaTeX local fallback at
     `../katex/` if math is set, otherwise plain markup — do not add a remote dependency.
   - **Homing** (§5) — the centrepiece. Cover: why an endstop at all; the fail-safe
     normally-closed convention with its truth table; debounce; the three-pass sequence as a
     table or a small diagram; why the slow third pass exists; the fault table.
   - **Electronics & control path** (§6) — expander, pin usage, driver, 12 V rail, the
     passive power-on safety, coil parking, the interlocks, the console commands table.
   - **Motion performance** (§7) — the 110 s → 22 s story with the before/after table, and
     where the remaining 22 s goes.
   - **Bench validation** (§8) — the results table, the honest limit on the repeatability
     figure, the stale-diagnostic bug, and the discarded contaminated run. Do not soften
     these; they are the most credible part of the record.
   - **Open questions & risks** (§9) and **next steps** (§10) — the stroke shortfall is the
     headline risk and should read as one.
   - **Version log** (§11) and **source documents** (§12).
6. A TOC using the shared `.proto-toc` component.
7. Link `PROTOTYPE.md` as the source document. Do **not** link `Alignment_Module_V2.mp4` —
   it is ~36 MB and re-encoding is deferred.
8. Where a claim carries a caveat in the source, carry the caveat. Specifically: the
   repeatability figure is **"no worse than ~0.03 mm"**, not "0.02 mm" — the measurement sits
   at the resolution floor of the method, and the page must say so.
