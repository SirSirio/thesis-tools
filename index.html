Peristaltic Rotor Geometry Solver

Analytically solves rotor radius for peristaltic pumps. Set your target stroke volume, tube ID, roller bearing, and occlusion efficiency — get a full geometry table for 3–12 rollers with feasibility checks.

Open tool→
🧫

Peristaltic Occlusion & Displaced-Volume Model

Two-part geometric reference for tube-deformation mechanics. Part 1: stadium cross-section model with interactive SVG — residual lumen area vs. roller squeeze. Part 2: axial contact length, displaced volume per roller pass, and arc compensation formula. Full derivations with LaTeX formulas.

Open tool→
〰

Tensioned Tube-Path & Stroke Volume Model

Alternative path geometry for a tube pinned at both ends under seating tension — straight roller-to-roller runs with α/2 wraps instead of the 180° wall arc. Includes axial pre-strain corrections (lumen shrink, interference loss), volume-per-stroke prediction, and a closed-form corrected rotor radius.
