  • Two parameters reached the build wrong: the rotor radius and the tube wall. Shimmed with folded paper it gave 3.39 µL of a nominal 5.
  • The inline flow sensor read a standard deviation of 1159 µL/min about a mean of 1104, and reverse flow once in seven samples.

  • Input queue at the left, dispensing lane in the middle, output tray at the right: the U the rack travels.

The reagent waits in a sealed vial, read by a sensor and drawn by two needles.

It arrived as a mesh, not a model, and that shaped the integration.
Designed and built by Marius Schiller.
  • The vial is inverted and sealed by a septum that recloses after the needle leaves.
  • Copper tape on the cartridge is the capacitive electrode; spring pins in the sleeve carry the signal out. Two-point calibration, within 5 %.
  • Two needles per vial: one draws the liquid, one vents the container through a 0.22 µm filter, so the air that replaces the reagent is sterile.
  • It came over as an uneditable mesh, so the carrier had to be designed around the physical hardware.
