The machine must dose 5 to 1000 µL of six liquids into forty tubes, on its own.

Design a portable liquid dispenser capable of delivering 5–1000 µL of up to six reagents across tens of 1.5 mL and 2 mL tubes, unattended, at a precision equal or superior to manual pipetting, operated at the point of need by non-specialist personnel.

Requirements are gates; the criteria only rank what already passed.

Six modules were built. The enclosure depends on all of them, and was not.

  • Read the design problem once, slowly; every number in it is measured, not chosen.
  • These six are gates: fail one and no score compensates.
  • The modules are ordered by what they commit: the pump first, the enclosure last.
  • And there they are on the machine, except the enclosure, which was never built.

Ask an AI to design a house and you see exactly where it fails.

the ask “Design a house.” Villa or townhouse: both valid, neither what was meant.
the edit “Add a window upstairs.” It lands where the balcony was agreed.
the memory An instruction from three sessions ago returns, and a wall moves.
the fix Set the layout first, align the floors, and check each one before raising the next.
  • Both houses answer the prompt; neither is the house that was wanted.
  • The window lands on the balcony because nothing recorded the balcony.
  • An old instruction resurfaces and silently moves a wall that was settled.
  • These are structural failures, so the answer is an environment, not better prompting.

So the AI worked inside written specs, a memory on disk, and checks it could not skip.
