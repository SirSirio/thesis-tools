  • This is the health check at the arrivals gate.
  • The officer takes a nasal swab. But confirming the virus means sending that tube to a central laboratory, and waiting.
  • Hold them a day and it is an operational nightmare; wave them through and an unconfirmed infection leaves the terminal. This axis is one day.
  • Standard molecular testing: more than 26 hours. And that is with the hospital's own lab on site.
  • A portable molecular test at the point of care: 2.6 hours.
  • If asked: both times come from one study, same hospital, same patients (Collier et al. 2020): a median of more than 26 hours from swab to result in the laboratory, 2.6 hours at the point of care.

Progetto PAIR – DTU Bioengineering

  • ~50 s
  • To diagnose the sick person at the gate, a protocol has been devised: PANPOC.
  • The swab goes into a tube that already holds liquid.
  • The cells are broken open, and the target is released.
  • A control is added.
  • The genetic material binds to magnetic beads.
  • A magnet holds them; the rest is removed.
  • Two washes.
  • It dries.
  • Water releases it.
  • Most of these steps are about liquid addition, while the others are process specific.
  • Thus, to generalise a process, a machine meant for liquid dispensing alone is sufficient.
  • If asked: PANPOC is the portable instrument of the Horizon Europe project PAIR, meant for sites such as airports and border controls. Its full preparation is about a dozen manual steps with six liquids, from 5 µL to 1 mL. The swab can arrive dry, in guanidine thiocyanate or in phosphate-buffered saline; from the bead step on the three are identical. The reagent of each stage is on appendix slide s04r.

PANPOC porta un test molecolare al gate.

  • Per la diagnosi al gate c’è un protocollo: PANPOC.
  • Il tampone va in una provetta già con liquido.
  • Le cellule si rompono e liberano ciò che si cerca.
  • Si aggiunge un controllo.
  • Il materiale genetico si lega alle biglie.
  • Un magnete le tiene; il resto si butta.
  • Due lavaggi.
  • Si asciuga.
  • Eluizione.
  • Quasi ogni passaggio aggiunge liquido; gli altri variano.
  • Quindi basta una macchina che dosa liquidi, per ogni protocollo.
