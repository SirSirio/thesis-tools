  • ~50 s
  • Four properties across the top.
  • The standard in every lab: the hand pipette.
  • Lab systems like the Tecan Fluent: precise and open to any protocol, but even the smallest is the size of a wardrobe.
  • Integrated platforms like the cobas liat: portable and unattended, but the volumes are fixed inside a cartridge, locked to one assay.
  • Open-source builds like the Sidekick: open and unattended, but calibrated on water and only partly portable.
  • Each family fails at least one property, and never the same one. The empty row is what the field needs — that is this machine.
  • If asked: the Fluent covers 0.5 to 1000 µL, but at half a microlitre its imprecision is about thirty times its best, and Tecan says calibration may be needed at and below 5 µL.
  • If asked: the cobas liat prices are not in the thesis — a resale price (Dipylon Medical) and the CADTH rapid flu review, about $100 a test, a 2015 figure.

La macchina è divisa in 8 moduli funzionali

  • ~40 s
  • Mapping the operating sequence gave eight functions, so eight modules.
  • Storage, pump, nozzle, alignment, tube holding, electronics, user interface and the enclosure: one function each.
  • The liquid runs one way: storage, pump, nozzle, into the tube. Tube holding was later absorbed into alignment.
  • Everything else between the modules is data and control.
  • Nothing wet crosses into the electronics. The enclosure stayed a specification: its shape depends on everything inside it.
  • If asked: reagent storage is Marius Schiller’s work, not one of my modules.

La macchina è divisa in 8 moduli funzionali

  • Otto funzioni, quindi otto moduli.
  • Ogni modulo fa una sola cosa.
  • Il liquido va in un solo verso; il supporto provette è finito nell’Allineamento.
  • Il resto sono dati e comandi.
  • Niente liquido nell’elettronica. L’involucro è rimasto sulla carta.

Affrontare i limiti dell’IA in ingegneria…

la richiesta “Progetta una casa.”
la modifica “Aggiungi una finestra sopra.”
la memoria Torna una vecchia istruzione.
la soluzione Specificare, poi verificare.
  • ~40 s
  • Used without constraints, AI fails in the same structural ways every time.
  • Say I ask it to design a house.
  • A villa or a townhouse: both valid, neither what I meant.
  • Now ask for a window upstairs: it lands half off the wall, because nothing written down said where the wall was.
  • Then an instruction from three sessions ago comes back, a load-bearing wall moves, and the roof comes with it.
  • As in all engineering, work with AI has to proceed in steps, each one written down and checked before the next.

Affrontare i limiti dell’IA in ingegneria…

  • Senza regole, l’IA sbaglia sempre negli stessi modi.
  • Le chiedo di progettare una casa.
  • Villa o schiera: entrambe valide, nessuna quella voluta.
  • Una finestra in più finisce fuori dal muro: nessuno aveva scritto dov’era.
  • Torna un vecchio ordine: il muro portante si sposta, e il tetto con lui.
  • Si procede a passi, ognuno scritto e verificato.
