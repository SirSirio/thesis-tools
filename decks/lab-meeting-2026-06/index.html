proto-01: 5 µL 4-roller baseline

Purpose: Prove the concept, validate the model, learn before next cycle.

  • Target 5.0 µL/stroke, 4 rollers, design interference δ = 0.20 mm
  • Issue: Gap was 1.75 mm (no natural occlusion); needed paper shim (δ_eff ≈ 0.6–0.8 mm)
  • Measured result: ≈3.4 µL/stroke (gravimetric), −32% error
  • Finding: Model predicts the shimmed result within ~11%

proto-02: Gap-Sweep Redesign

Correction & characterization build to hit spec and lock the gap.

  • Corrected N_c = 2 (Rotor R: 17.7 → 19.7 mm)
  • Measured gap sweep: 1.52 / 1.62 / 1.72 mm to locate the seal peak
  • Head lock: Screw clamp + 0.15–0.20 mm sliding fit for repeatability
  • Tests: Determine true tube wall (w) and inflation factor (k)
