Iteration

Gap-sweep approach maps the optimal seal

  • Delivery is leak-limited, not arc-limited.
  • Sweeping 3 distinct head geometries (1.52, 1.62, 1.72 mm).
  • Hunting the peak: the loosest gap that fully seals.
Test-campaign app

One app runs the campaign — I just weigh the tubes

  • Set the DoE parameters — the app designs & randomizes the run order.
  • Commands the Arduino directly: dose & parameters per run — no re-flashing.
  • Runs unattended — I just collect the tubes and weigh them.
  • Randomized order → statistically robust results, far faster.
